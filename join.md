We aim to keep a supportive research environment that leaves room for creativity and
the freedom to explore.

- We want our research to be robust and reproducible.
- We commit to open-access publications and to sharing research data as soon as we can.
- We commit to honesty and respect, and to helping each other and other researchers.
- We aim for discoveries and new methods. We follow what is interesting and we are not
  afraid to fail.
- We welcome passionate scientists and students of all genders and nationalities, and
  anyone who needs extra support to follow their scientific passion.

## PhD positions

We currently do not have funded PhD positions, but we accept applications from students
with funding from their own government or from another funding organisation.

## Other funded PhD positions

We accept students with funding from sources such as the China Scholarship Council and
Commonwealth PhD funding. Please [get in touch]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) for
more information.

## Postdocs and visiting researchers

If your interests overlap with ours, email a short description of what you would like to
work on along with your CV. We are happy to support fellowship applications.
